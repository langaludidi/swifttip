# SwiftTip MVP v3 — Pricing Decision Pack

Status: **WORKING COMMERCIAL REVIEW / NOT APPROVED**

Pricing version: `v3-working-001`

This pack turns the eight pricing blockers into an evidence checklist. It does not approve, schedule, activate or publish pricing and does not enable payments.

## Current working hypothesis

- Worker Success Fee: 5% of the gratuity.
- Customer Service Fee: R1.00 plus 3% of the gratuity, capped at R5.00.
- Minimum gratuity: R5.00.
- Maximum gratuity: R500.00.
- High-value review threshold: R200.00.

These figures are hypotheses only. The Admin pricing page calculates minimum, common, high-value and maximum scenarios from the stored draft so reviewers can compare one economic truth.

## Approval evidence rule

A blocker may be removed only when its evidence reference, decision owner, decision date and approved wording or numeric input are recorded. Verbal agreement, a screenshot without provenance or an assumed provider price is insufficient.

Pricing approval remains separate from:

- setting an effective date;
- scheduling or activating a Pricing Version;
- publishing legal terms;
- enabling public Tip intake;
- configuring a payment provider; and
- moving real money.
