# SwiftTip Venue Terms — Draft v0.1

**Status: DRAFT / NOT PUBLISHED / NOT IN FORCE**

This document is a pre-live drafting instrument for legal, commercial and operational review. It must not be presented to a Venue as operative terms until an approved, published and effective version exists in the SwiftTip legal-version system.

## 1. Parties and scope

These Venue Terms govern participation by an approved business or operating location in SwiftTip.

- **SwiftTip** means the digital gratuity platform operated by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME TO BE CONFIRMED]**.
- **Venue** means the approved business or site recorded in SwiftTip.
- **Venue User** means a person authorised to access the Venue surface.
- **Worker** means a person using a SwiftTip Worker profile whose relationship with the Venue may be confirmed through the platform.
- **Customer** means a person who chooses to make a digital gratuity through SwiftTip.
- **Payment Provider** means the approved third-party payment service provider responsible for the live payment and Settlement mechanics.

These Terms are deliberately limited to Venue participation in SwiftTip. They do not appoint the Venue as payroll administrator, Worker funds custodian or Settlement operator unless a later expressly approved model says otherwise.

## 2. Venue participation model

The intended MVP commercial model does not require a mandatory recurring Venue subscription.

The Venue participates principally by:

1. allowing SwiftTip to identify the Venue accurately;
2. confirming authorised Venue Users;
3. confirming or declining Worker–Venue relationships;
4. ending Worker associations when they cease to be current; and
5. cooperating reasonably with operational, fraud or support enquiries concerning the Venue relationship.

SwiftTip may later offer optional paid Venue services under separate commercial terms. No such service is created by these Terms unless expressly agreed.

## 3. Venue approval and access

A Venue does not self-authorise itself into the live SwiftTip network.

SwiftTip Operations creates and reviews the Venue record. Venue access is invitation-based. Authentication alone does not grant a person access to Venue data.

A Venue User must:

- use their own authorised account;
- protect authentication credentials;
- act within the role assigned to them; and
- promptly report suspected unauthorised access or a change in authority.

The Venue is responsible for ensuring that persons acting as Venue Users are appropriately authorised by the Venue.

## 4. Worker confirmation

The Venue’s principal Worker-related responsibility is narrow: confirm whether the person shown currently works at, is contracted to, or otherwise lawfully provides the relevant service at the Venue in the stated role.

The Venue is **not** responsible through SwiftTip for:

- approving SwiftTip identity verification;
- reviewing Worker identity documents;
- reviewing Worker banking or Payment Provider KYC;
- deciding Worker tax treatment;
- operating Worker Settlement; or
- determining whether a Worker is an employee, contractor or other legal category.

The Venue must not confirm a Worker it knows or reasonably believes does not have the stated current relationship with the Venue.

## 5. Ending Worker associations

The Venue should end a SwiftTip Venue association promptly when the relevant Worker relationship ends or the Worker should no longer receive new gratuities under that Venue identity.

Ending the association:

- prevents new SwiftTip activity relying on that Venue association once the platform applies the change;
- does not erase historical transactions;
- does not itself determine the Worker’s broader legal or employment rights; and
- does not authorise the Venue to control historical Worker Settlement records.

## 6. No payroll, wage or tip-pool administration

The MVP is designed for **individual voluntary gratuities to identified Workers**.

SwiftTip does not, through these Terms, provide:

- payroll processing;
- wage calculation;
- mandatory service-charge allocation;
- tip-pool allocation;
- shift-based tip distribution;
- deduction administration; or
- employer-controlled Worker payout batches.

If a future product introduces any of these functions, it requires separate legal, product and commercial approval.

## 7. Venue visibility and privacy boundaries

The Venue surface may provide operational information such as:

- participating Worker names and roles;
- association status;
- aggregate participation metrics; and
- limited Venue-level activity required to support the service.

The Venue must not receive through the ordinary Venue interface:

- Worker identity documents;
- full Worker KYC records;
- Worker bank account or Settlement-destination details;
- private phone numbers unless separately justified and authorised;
- individual earnings rankings or leaderboards; or
- unnecessary customer personal information.

The Venue must use any SwiftTip information it can access only for authorised Venue participation purposes and in accordance with applicable privacy law.

## 8. Customer experience at the Venue

The Venue must not represent a SwiftTip gratuity as compulsory if it is presented to the Customer as a voluntary tip.

The Venue must not require Workers to misstate the identity of the recipient, redirect one Worker’s tipping code to another person, or create misleading signage or payment instructions.

Where SwiftTip materials are displayed at the Venue, the Venue should take reasonable care not to alter or obscure material fee, identity or safety information.

## 9. Fees and payment relationship

The Venue is not charged merely because a Customer tips a Worker under the current MVP hypothesis.

Customer and Worker transaction fees, if any, are governed by the approved Pricing Version and the applicable Customer and Worker terms, not by undisclosed deductions imposed by the Venue.

The final live Payment Provider, merchant relationship and settlement contracting structure are not yet approved. These Terms must not be published until the Venue’s actual position in the payment chain is confirmed.

## 10. No custody of Worker gratuity funds

The intended architecture does not require the Venue to receive, pool or redistribute the Worker’s SwiftTip gratuity principal.

The Venue must not represent to Workers or Customers that SwiftTip requires the Venue to hold Worker gratuity balances unless an expressly approved future model introduces such a role.

## 11. Fraud, misuse and cooperation

The Venue must not knowingly facilitate:

- fabricated Worker identities;
- sham or self-funded transactions designed to manufacture activity;
- QR-code substitution or redirection;
- use of SwiftTip for unlawful payments;
- coercive tipping practices; or
- attempts to bypass payment, identity or audit controls.

The Venue should cooperate reasonably with SwiftTip where a transaction, Worker association or security incident genuinely requires Venue confirmation.

SwiftTip should not request unrelated employment or personal information merely because a support case exists.

## 12. Brand and materials

SwiftTip may permit the Venue to display approved QR materials, Worker tipping identifiers, explanatory signage or SwiftTip branding.

The Venue may not materially alter SwiftTip branding in a way that misleads Customers or Workers about fees, recipient identity, payment status or the relationship between the Venue and SwiftTip.

Any formal trademark licence or co-marketing rights beyond ordinary pilot use must be separately documented.

## 13. Suspension of Venue access

SwiftTip may suspend or restrict Venue access where reasonably necessary for:

- account compromise;
- material misuse;
- fraudulent Worker confirmations;
- data-protection concerns;
- Payment Provider requirements;
- legal or regulatory obligations; or
- protection of Customers or Workers.

Where practicable and lawful, SwiftTip will communicate the reason and any remediation steps.

A platform-wide payment kill switch may stop new live payments without deleting historical Venue, Worker or transaction records.

## 14. Records and auditability

SwiftTip may retain auditable records of:

- Venue creation and approval;
- Venue User invitations and roles;
- Worker association decisions;
- terms acceptance;
- support and security actions; and
- other material events needed to operate and defend the integrity of the service.

The Venue must not request deletion of records that SwiftTip is legally required or reasonably entitled to retain for transaction, dispute, fraud, audit or compliance purposes.

## 15. Privacy and data protection

Each party must comply with applicable South African privacy and data-protection obligations for personal information for which it is responsible.

The final allocation of responsible-party/operator roles for specific data exchanges must be confirmed before publication, particularly where a Payment Provider or external communications provider processes personal information.

The SwiftTip Privacy Notice will describe SwiftTip’s own processing activities and data-subject rights.

## 16. Service availability

SwiftTip may perform maintenance, security changes and controlled releases. Continuous availability is not guaranteed.

The Venue should not rely on SwiftTip as its sole payroll, employment, emergency or statutory record system.

## 17. Liability framework — draft position

Each party remains responsible for its own acts and omissions.

The Venue remains responsible for the accuracy of confirmations made by its authorised Venue Users. SwiftTip remains responsible for operating its platform with reasonable care and maintaining the controls it represents as part of the service.

The final limitation-of-liability, indemnity and third-party-service clauses require South African legal review and must not exclude liability or statutory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 18. Electronic contracting

Venue Users may accept these Terms electronically on behalf of the Venue only if they are authorised to do so.

SwiftTip records the exact version accepted, the relevant Venue membership, time of acceptance and audit evidence. The accepted version must be available for later review.

## 19. Changes to these Terms

Published Venue Terms are versioned. SwiftTip will not silently change the content of an accepted published version.

A material change requires a new version and may require fresh acceptance before continued Venue participation.

## 20. Termination

Either party may end future participation subject to any agreed notice arrangement in the final version.

Termination does not automatically erase historical transaction, association, audit, support, dispute or legal-acceptance records.

The treatment of branded materials, active Worker associations and pending operational cases on termination must be addressed in the final operating procedure.

## 21. Governing law and disputes

The intended governing law is the law of the Republic of South Africa, subject to mandatory rights and jurisdiction that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Final notice addresses, dispute-escalation procedure and external complaint mechanisms must be confirmed before publication.
